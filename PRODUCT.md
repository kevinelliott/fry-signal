## Users

- Players who want a fast, tactile restaurant-management game that is satisfying before any wallet is connected.
- Returning players who want to improve their operational judgment, restore a distinctive roadside restaurant, and optionally preserve selected earned keepsakes.
- Keyboard, touch, and pointer players across mobile and desktop web layouts.

## Product Purpose

Fry Signal is a retro-future fried-chicken drive-through game about reading traffic, choosing batch risk, routing work, packing accurately, and recovering from a rush without wasting food or exhausting the crew. A successful first release lets a new player complete and understand a fun walletless shift in five to eight minutes.

Restaurant profit is a health signal inside the fiction: enough margin keeps the lights on, pays the crew fairly, absorbs recovery choices, and funds restoration. Financial return, token appreciation, and speculative yield are not player promises or progression goals.

## Positioning

The signature mechanism is a live routing board that lets the player rewire hot, cold, drink, and dispatch paths while traffic arrives in forecast waves. The player conducts flow and freshness rather than repeating a generic cooking-timer loop.

Within the Open Sign Games family, Fry Signal owns burst throughput, traffic forecasting, lane topology, crew handoffs, and recoverable bottlenecks. It does not inherit another restaurant's HUD, pacing, scoring, progression, or economy.

## Operating Context

- A shift alternates short planning lulls with intense one-to-two-minute traffic waves.
- The canonical player decisions are `read -> commit -> switch -> recover`. Ordinary batching, packing, and dispatch visualize the consequences of those decisions; they do not become separate repetitive minigames.
- The first vertical is fully local and walletless.
- Ordinary chain development targets a loopback-only Tezos Localnet. Shadownet is an explicit exact-candidate rehearsal. Mainnet is absent from normal development profiles.
- Tezos may later become authoritative for selected transferable ownership and provenance. Service play, timing, scoring, quests, restoration, and non-transferable progression remain fast game authority.
- Phase 1 is locally authoritative and network-free. Shared persistence is deferred until multi-device or account play earns it; scarce or transferable eligibility requires a separately disclosed server-verified replay and can never inherit authority from a client-only result.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Working title: **Fry Signal**. Working line: **When it lights, move.** Service line: **Build the batch. Route the rush.**
- Menu identity centers fried chicken and road-trip comfort food: sandwiches, tenders, waffle fries, slaw, biscuits, hand pies, soft serve, shakes, and authored sauce flights.
- Progression expands lane geometry, crew specialties, windows, bus bays, dessert spurs, signage, and roadside stories. It does not sell raw speed or relief from service pressure.
- The first playable proves one night, three meal families, one focal regular, one crew teaching voice, three announced waves, and a continuous routing board. The broader cast, menu, bus bay, dessert spur, and multi-location breadth are deferred until the board is replayable.
- The first session must remain complete with every crypto noun removed.
- Wallet actions must be deliberate and preview network, exact inputs and outputs, permanence, fees, provenance, and transferability.
- `submitted`, `applied`, and `failed` are distinct states. Submission is never represented as ownership or confirmation.
- Cross-title recognition is allowlisted and cosmetic or commemorative. There is no umbrella currency, shared power, arbitrary NFT ingestion, or token-gated core play.
- No investment, appreciation, APY, rarity, or universal-ownership claim may drive product copy.
- Recovery restores otherwise-lost service credit and always has a bounded visible cost. Intentionally creating a recoverable fault can never improve score, health, progression, keepsake eligibility, or rarity.
- The tutorial teaches recovery through an announced constraint or unscored beat, never a surprise machine failure inside a scored wave.
- Fry Signal still requires formal exact and phonetic trademark, storefront, company, domain, social-handle, and counsel-led review before public brand commitment.

## Brand Commitments

- Open Sign Games presents Fry Signal; the masterbrand introduces rather than prefixes or absorbs the title.
- The restaurant is the dependable night stop at Mile 42, where city streets, farms, freight routes, and the coast road meet after dark. The player's emotional job is to turn an unpredictable road into a place people can count on.
- The world is a retro-future roadside fried-chicken drive-through with sun-faded daytime surfaces, neon and taillight energy at night, a sequential chicken marquee, steam bursts, chunky traveling-bag sprites, and shape-plus-label operational signals.
- A chicken-shaped three-lamp signal unifies the roadside marquee and operational language: `batch / pack / go`.
- Family resemblance is limited to handcrafted pixel discipline, a restrained four-pixel corner notch, accessibility primitives, and truthful optional ownership.
- The interface must remain identifiable in grayscale by its routing-board and vehicle/window topology.
- Voice is warm, quick, humane, and operationally playful. It never glamorizes burnout, treats labor as disposable, or turns revenue into hustle culture.
- Fair staffing, paid work, breaks, and safe equipment limits are fixed house commitments. Crew members are people and story collaborators, never collectible capacity upgrades or economic levers.

## Product Principles

1. Play earns attention before ownership asks for it.
2. Flow decisions create the fun; timers only make those decisions legible.
3. Every failure opens a recovery choice instead of erasing scarce property.
4. Restaurant health supports people and place, not an investment pitch.
5. Chain truth, application truth, and deployment proof stay explicit and separate.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

- Essential states use shape, position, label, and motion together; color is never the only signal.
- Support keyboard, touch, and pointer parity, visible focus, at least 44px targets, reduced-motion equivalents, and 200% zoom.
- The first playable vertical must work at 320px, 390px, 768px, and desktop widths without hidden essential actions or horizontal overflow.
- Crew writing and mechanics avoid exploitative labor framing, cultural flattening, and punishment for taking humane recovery actions.
