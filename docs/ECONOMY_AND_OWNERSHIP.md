# Economy and Ownership

## Product rule

**Throughput is gameplay; ownership is memory.** Fry Signal has no `FRY` currency, does not use DER as operating money, and never converts restaurant margin into player yield. Live traffic, batches, freshness, routing, recovery, revenue, costs, progression, and restaurant health remain fast game authority.

## Two launch ledgers, one deferred ledger

| Ledger | Authority | Transferable | Contents |
| --- | --- | ---: | --- |
| Shift | Phase 1 local deterministic reducer; later server replay where disclosed | No | forecast, orders, batches, freshness, routes, waits, waste, recovery, costs, results |
| Restaurant | local account state; later shared account authority if approved | No | progression, stories, topology choices, restoration, accessibility/preferences, Operating Reserve |
| Signal Fixture | Tezos only after confirmation; deferred | Policy-defined | one allowlisted class of cosmetic roadside expression |

Wallet linkage is optional, reversible account state. A signature proves wallet control for that action; it does not make the wallet the player account or replace the first two ledgers.

## Restaurant economics

The House Book uses transparent integer-cent arithmetic:

`operating margin = sales − ingredients − waste/remakes − recovery/make-goods − maintenance − fixed fair staffing`

Fair staffing, wages, breaks, and safe equipment limits are fixed commitments. They cannot be cut to improve a score. Margin is one constraint within restaurant health alongside accuracy, warmth, flow, guest care, and crew/equipment condition. Positive margin cannot cancel harmful service or unsafe operation.

Positive margin enters an account-bound **Operating Reserve** for visible restaurant reinvestment: route topology, accessibility improvements, maintenance, authored menu programs, story chapters, signage, and crew comfort. The reserve is not a token; it cannot be purchased, transferred, cashed out, pledged, redeemed, or denominated in tez. A shortfall changes planning choices without layoffs, wage cuts, erased story progress, or a grind spiral.

Recovery restores otherwise-lost service credit and posts its cost once. Intentionally causing a recoverable fault can never improve margin, Service Signal, progression, postcard output, fixture eligibility, or any other reward.

## First possible crypto proof: Signal Fixture

All crypto is deferred until the walletless gray-box game passes. If ownership is then justified, the first proof is one narrowly scoped **Signal Fixture** flow—such as an authored marquee face, window sign, lane pennant, signal housing, bag wrap, or approved chime.

Requirements:

- cosmetic expression only; no effect on timing, capacity, freshness, forecast accuracy, queue tolerance, score, recovery, margin, or unlock pace;
- exact authored selection before signature; no paid randomness or mystery result;
- no paid mint in the first proof;
- explicit supply, transfer policy, admin powers, metadata digest, and incident policy;
- install/uninstall is ordinary offchain account configuration, not a chain transaction;
- ownership is recognized only after confirmed canonical chain evidence.

Do not add a Night Dispatch Card, second asset class, DER compatibility, foreign assets, tokenized food, recipes, orders, crew, revenue, Operating Reserve, score, or progression to the first ownership proof.

## Deliberate ownership flow

If approved after Phase 1:

1. Link a wallet outside the shift.
2. Verify ownership or review a named fixture action.
3. Show network, exact `chain_id + contract + token_id`, action, fee, attached mutez, supply/transfer policy, public permanence, and metadata.
4. Request signature only after review.
5. Track awaiting signature → submitted → included → confirmed/finalized, plus failed/dropped/replaced/reorged.
6. Install only confirmed exact ownership; reorg rolls the projection back truthfully.

Submission is not ownership. An indexer result, ticker, image, name, or wallet claim never authorizes an asset.

## Eligibility boundary

Phase 1 local results have no scarce or transferable eligibility. A future fixture entitlement must come from a separately disclosed server-verified mode whose server issues the authority inputs and replays bounded ordered commands. Wallet cycling, duplicate submission, recovery farming, high margin, high score, long streak, resale volume, or wallet wealth cannot multiply or improve eligibility.

## Prohibited mechanics and claims

- no umbrella currency, yield, APY, staking, presale, buyback, bonding curve, referral emission, or token-gated story;
- no loot box, valuable randomness, burn-to-upgrade, rentals, collateral, token crafting, or marketplace requirement;
- no floor price, portfolio value, ROI, rarity-power, scarcity countdown, ticker, volume reward, or public wallet-value ranking;
- no ownership prompt in the first shift and no chain confirmation blocking service;
- no cross-title asset with power, inventory, recipe, or optimization value;
- no mistake, spoilage, refund, recovery, or failed shift destroying or mutating an owned asset.

## Release gates

1. The complete first shift and progression work with no wallet, network, or crypto noun.
2. Owning every fixture produces identical gameplay and economic opportunity to owning none.
3. Localnet proves exact identity, preview, reject, submit, confirm, inspect, install, unlink/relink, duplicate handling, drift, and reorg reconciliation before Shadownet.
4. Any paid issuance, Mainnet use, marketplace link, or broader interoperability is a separate legal, security, economic-abuse, consumer-truth, and go/no-go decision.
